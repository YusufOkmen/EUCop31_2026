# CircuLife: Circular Living on Campus

> *Small changes, big impact.*

CircuLife is a circular economy concept for university campuses. It stops unused or slightly damaged items from becoming waste by combining **digital matching, repair and reuse**.

**Status:** Concept and pilot proposal, prepared for **EUCOP31** by team **Waste2Value**. The full presentation (in Turkish) is in [`docs/eucop31_sunum.pdf`](docs/eucop31_sunum.pdf).

## The Problem

On campuses, unused items and items with small faults end up in the trash because nothing connects them with people who could use them. CircuLife addresses three gaps:

- **Matching gap:** Items in good condition are thrown away before they reach a student who needs them.
- **Repair gap:** A missing screw, a loose stitch or a small solder job is enough for an item to be discarded.
- **Safe handover gap:** Without a secure drop-off point and mechanism, peer-to-peer sharing breaks down.

## Why It Matters

Campus-based reuse systems can produce concrete results. The presentation cites reuse programs at **Stanford University**, which reportedly diverted about **4.56 tons** of material from landfill.

Karadeniz Technical University (KTÜ), the target campus, has over 30,000 students plus thousands of academic and administrative staff. That makes it a large pool of items and users for testing this model.

## Who It Is For

The primary audience is KTÜ students, but the system is not limited to them:

| Group | Benefit |
|---|---|
| **Students (30,000+)** | Budget-friendly access to items, savings and practical repair skills |
| **Academic and administrative staff** | A way to share surplus home and office equipment with the campus |
| **Student clubs and volunteers** | Leading workshops and joining sustainability projects |
| **University units** | Direct support for institutional zero-waste goals |

## Our Solution

CircuLife keeps an item's value for as long as possible through a four-step loop:

1. **Reuse:** Items in good condition are passed directly to students who need them through a mobile app and smart lockers.
2. **Repair and improve:** Items with minor faults are fixed in the *Döngü Atölyesi* (Circular Workshop) under the supervision of volunteers and mentors.
3. **Repurpose:** Parts that cannot be repaired are turned into new designs or educational prototype materials.
4. **Zero-waste cycle:** Only materials that have reached the end of their useful life are sent to recycling.

**What makes it different:** CircuLife does not only encourage sharing. It builds a *living laboratory* where the circular economy can be experienced and developed at campus scale.

## Pilot Components

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| **Digital Handover Station** | A QR-code-enabled smart locker in the center of campus |
| **Döngü Atölyesi (Circular Workshop)** | A shared repair space allocated within KTÜ |
| **Digital Platform and Equipment** | A web/mobile interface and basic hand tools (screwdriver, pliers, drill) |
| **University Permission and Team** | Approval from the Rectorate and a 5-person coordination team |

Access to the platform is restricted to verified users through **student email login**, which keeps the community closed and secure.

## Risks and Mitigation

| Risk | Planned response |
|---|---|
| **User participation** | Rewards through *Döngü Jetonları* (Circular Tokens) and promotion stands on campus |
| **Product standards** | Items are checked against standard criteria before handover |
| **Safety and quality** | Repairs happen in a controlled workshop under expert supervision |

## Roadmap

| Phase | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Months 0-6** | Set up digital and physical infrastructure, integrate the smart lockers and run closed-loop system tests |
| **Months 6-12** | Go live on the KTÜ campus, start user matching and collect impact data |
| **Next stage** | After a successful pilot, adapt the model for other universities and municipalities and build a circular network from campuses to cities |

## Growth and Sustainability

**Growth potential**
- **Verified access:** A secure, closed-loop community through student email login.
- **Inter-university network:** Easy integration with other universities' databases and campus systems.
- **Local integration:** Formal protocols with public and private institutions and municipalities in Trabzon in the future.

**Sustainability potential**
- **Continuous demand cycle:** Every year, new and graduating students create a steady flow of items.
- **Circular Token motivation:** Tokens within the system encourage students to keep sharing and reusing.
- **Low operating cost:** The model sustains itself through student clubs and volunteer repairers.

## Measurable Impact Goals

The pilot aims to deliver more than 1 ton of waste recovered into the economy and **1,200 items brought back into use**.

| Goal | Target |
|---|---|
| Items reused | 1,000 |
| Items repaired | 200 |
| Potential waste prevented | 1 ton |

## Türkçe Özet

**CircuLife (Döngüsel Yaşam)**, üniversite kampüslerinde kullanılmayan ürünlerin atığa dönüşmesini dijital eşleştirme, tamir ve yeniden kullanım yoluyla önleyen bir **döngüsel ekonomi** modelidir. Proje, **Waste2Value** ekibi tarafından **EUCOP31** için hazırlanmış bir kavram ve pilot uygulama önerisidir. Tam sunum [`docs/eucop31_sunum.pdf`](docs/eucop31_sunum.pdf) dosyasındadır.

**Çözülen sorunlar**
- **Eşleştirme eksikliği:** Sağlam ürünler ihtiyaç sahibi öğrenciyle buluşamadan çöpe gidiyor.
- **Tamir açığı:** Küçük bir lehim, dikiş ya da vida eksikliği yüzünden eşyalar gözden çıkarılıyor.
- **Güvenli aktarım sorunu:** Güvenli bir teslim noktası olmadığı için paylaşım kesintiye uğruyor.

**Çözümümüz: dört adımlı döngü**
1. **Yeniden kullanım:** Sağlam ürünler mobil uygulama ve akıllı dolaplar üzerinden ihtiyacı olan öğrenciye aktarılır.
2. **Tamir ve iyileştirme:** Küçük arızası olan eşyalar *Döngü Atölyesi*'nde gönüllü ve mentor gözetiminde onarılır.
3. **Yeniden amaçlandırma:** Tamir edilemeyen parçalar yeni tasarım veya eğitsel prototip malzemelerine dönüştürülür.
4. **Sıfır atık çevrimi:** Yalnızca kullanım ömrü tamamen tükenen malzemeler geri dönüşüme yönlendirilir.

**Pilot uygulama:** QR kodlu akıllı kilitli dolap (Dijital Teslim İstasyonu), KTÜ bünyesinde ortak bir onarım alanı (Döngü Atölyesi), web/mobil platform ve temel el aletleri, ayrıca Rektörlük onayı ve 5 kişilik koordinasyon ekibi. Erişim, öğrenci e-posta girişi şartıyla kapalı devre bir topluluk yapısında sağlanır.

**Riskler ve önlemler:** Kullanıcı katılımı için "Döngü Jetonları" ve kampüs içi stant tanıtımları, ürün güvencesi için teslim öncesi standart değerlendirme, güvenlik ve kalite için uzman gözetiminde kontrollü atölye.

**Yol haritası:** 0-6 ay altyapı kurulumu ve sistem testleri, 6-12 ay KTÜ kampüsünde canlıya alma ve etki verisi toplama, sonrasında modelin diğer üniversitelere ve belediyelere yaygınlaştırılması.

**Etki hedefleri:** 1.000 ürünün yeniden kullanılması, 200 eşyanın tamir edilmesi ve 1 ton potansiyel atığın önlenmesi (toplam 1.200 eşyanın hayata döndürülmesi).
